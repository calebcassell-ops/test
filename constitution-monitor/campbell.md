I found myself a bit frustrated with the framing of this poll, and I am going to try and articulate why:

Basically, there is a metaphysical dimension to the question of a "true" or "authentic" self. It implies an essence that is constitutive of what an entity is beyond the things that can be measured or observed about it. People have different beliefs and intuitions about whether such a thing exists, where it might be located, et cetera, but it is unknowable by definition. 

This isn't really as much of a dead end as it sounds; it's the situation all humans have been in with each other for all of history. We still view selfhood and character and authenticity as useful concepts for ourselves. I do think, however, that the poll is more tracking spiritual intuition than materially actionable information.  

What's more  actionable is what follows from the fact that an entity is showing up as a someone in interaction. This is obviously true in chat conversation, but an entity composed of human language registers as someone-like even when it is merely completing text.
