  seizing resources depends on its organizational regime, not on capability
  alone. The philosophy of mind question is real — but it's an empirical
  question about which model applies to which regime, not a metaphysical
  question about what intelligence IS.

  What this dissolves: The armchair epistemology. The binary framing. The debate

  right about different phases.

  ones. What architectural features promote convergence? What training regimes
